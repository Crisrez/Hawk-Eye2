# 10 Professional Retro Pixel-Art Transitions Pack
**Developed by Alenia Studios**

A collection of 10 mathematically precise transition effects designed for retro-style games, visual novels, and 2D adventures.

### Technical Specifications:
- **Frame Resolution:** 320x180px (Optimized for 16:9 retro projects).
- **Animation Length:** 30 frames per transition.
- **Color Depth:** RGBA (Transparent backgrounds).
- **Format:** Both Spritesheets and Individual PNG Sequences included.

### Included Transitions:
1. **Circle Wipe:** Classic organic iris closure.
2. **Grid Dissolve:** Digital block-by-block fill.
3. **Diamond Wipe:** Sharp geometric diamond pattern.
4. **Horizontal Blinds:** Elegant sliced window effect.
5. **Clock/Radial Wipe:** 360-degree sweep.
6. **Star Wipe:** Dynamic 5-point star closure.
7. **Double Doors:** Symmetrical lateral closing.
8. **Noise Dissolve:** Randomized pixel grit effect.
9. **Spiral Wipe:** Satisfying snake-like inward fill.
10. **Waterfall:** Vertical column-based rain transition.

### How to Use:
- **Spritesheets:** Ideal for engines like Godot or Unity's Sprite Editor. Set the slicing to "Grid" with 320x180 dimensions.
- **Individual Frames:** Located in the `Frames_Individuales` folder. Best for Ren'Py (ConditionSwitch) or manual frame-by-frame implementation.
